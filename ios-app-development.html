What iOS app development includes

An iOS app is more than a set of screens on an iPhone. It needs a clear first-version scope, a design that feels at home on Apple devices, a backend to store accounts and data, an admin panel so your team is not dependent on a developer for everyday changes, and a careful App Store submission. I handle that path as one piece of work, so you are not coordinating three vendors who each own a different part of the product.

The work starts with a clickable demo or prototype. You can try the main flow on a phone before development begins in earnest. After you approve the direction, the build is split into milestones that you can see and test.

Native iOS or a shared Android app

This is the first honest decision. A native iOS app, usually built with Swift, is a good fit when the product is iPhone-first, needs deep access to Apple features, or must feel indistinguishable from the apps your customers already use. A cross-platform build is a better fit when you also need Android and want one product team and one backlog.

I will not push a stack that wastes your budget. If your customers in India are mostly on Android, starting with iOS alone can be the wrong order. If your buyers in the USA or the UAE are strongly on iPhone, an iOS-quality first release can be the right move. The combined app development service covers both stores when you need them together.

App Store, privacy and payments

Apple reviews every new app and many updates. That review looks at what the app does, how it uses personal data, how payments work and whether it behaves reliably. Privacy nutrition labels, permission wording and account deletion rules need to be correct. If you sell inside the app, Apple’s payment rules have to be followed. This is ordinary, plannable work when it is done early, and a source of delays when it is left to the week of launch.

The listing itself is created in your Apple Developer account. You own the name, the reviews and the relationship with Apple.

Backends, admin panels and later updates

Most business apps need sign-in, profiles, notifications and a way for staff to manage content or orders. That backend is part of the same project, and it can also serve a website or a future Android app. UI/UX design can be included first if the flows are still being decided.

After launch, small fixes are part of the agreed support window. New features are quoted as clear follow-on milestones, with the code already in your repository.

Built for India, the USA and the UAE

Apps in these markets often need more than a translated label. Payments, phone numbers, address formats and push-notification timing change by country. Some products need English plus Hindi; others need to feel native to a US or UAE audience from the first screen. We plan for the countries you actually want customers in, rather than shipping a single generic experience and hoping it travels.

If you have an app idea or an existing iOS product that needs a cleaner rebuild, message Anmol Gupta with a short brief. You will get a straight recommendation and a free direction demo before you commit to a full build.

FAQ

iOS App Development questions

Do you build native Swift apps or cross-platform apps?

Both are options. If you need a polished iOS-only product, native development is often the cleanest path. If you also need Android from the same budget, a cross-platform approach can be the better value. I recommend based on your users, timeline and budget, not on a single stack.

I also need Android. Should I start with iOS only?

Only if most of your customers are on iPhone. Otherwise it is usually better to plan both from the start, even if we launch one store first. See the main app development page for combined Android and iOS work.

Who owns the Apple Developer account?

You do. The app is published from your account, and you keep the certificates, listings and source code. I work as your developer, not as the owner of the listing.

How long does App Store review take?

Apple often reviews a new app in a few days, but it can take longer, and they may request changes. I prepare privacy details, screenshots and review notes carefully to reduce back-and-forth, and I stay with you until the app is live.

Can you update an existing iOS app?

Yes, if we can access the source code and the Apple account. I will review the current app first and tell you what is realistic before quoting.
