Why GEO and AEO exist

Search is no longer only ten blue links. Google may show an AI Overview above the results. A founder may ask ChatGPT for “a website developer in Delhi” or “an LMS for a training company in Dubai”. A researcher may use Perplexity and click the citations under the answer. If your site is vague, poorly structured or hard to attribute, those systems will quote someone else, even if you do good work.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the work of becoming a source that generative tools can trust and name. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the work of becoming the clear answer to a specific question. They share the same habits: write the answer in plain language, prove it with useful detail, mark up the page so machines can parse it, and make your organisation easy to identify.

What actually helps an AI cite you

Models look for text that answers the question directly, pages that stay consistent about the business name, location and services, and structured clues such as schema, FAQs and clean headings. They also look for corroboration: the same name, phone and service area on other reputable pages. Tricks, keyword stuffing and fake “AI citations” do not hold up and can hurt the site.

Practical work therefore looks like this:

  • Put a short, accurate definition near the top of each important page, then expand it.
  • Use one brand name consistently. On this site that name is Anmol Gupta.
  • Be explicit about where you work: India, the USA and the UAE, plus the city when it matters.
  • Add FAQ and Service schema that match the visible text, not hidden keyword lists.
  • Maintain an llms.txt file that summarises services and key URLs.
  • Keep technical SEO healthy: indexable pages, fast loads, a sitemap, and internal links that explain relationships between services.

How this differs from traditional SEO

You still want rankings, titles, internal links and good content. GEO and AEO add a different writing and structure standard. Pages should answer a question in the first screen, not hide the point under slogans. Statistics and process steps should be specific enough to quote. Brand and service names should be stable so an engine can form an entity, not a blur of old and new names.

It also means thinking about sources beyond your site: a Google Business Profile, professional directories, and articles that genuinely describe your work. That is related to, but not the same as, classic link building.

India, the USA and the UAE

AI answers are often local even when the interface looks global. A query about “CRM developers for a Dubai trading firm” or “Google Ads for a small business in Texas” will favour businesses that have said, clearly and consistently, that they serve that market. Content for these countries should use real service context, not a single generic paragraph with a list of place names bolted on.

Timelines and honest limits

AI systems take time to refresh what they know, and none of them publish a reliable “submit your site and appear next week” button. Some questions will never name a small firm, because the model prefers large publications. The win is to be the cited specialist for the questions you can actually earn: your services, your process, your geography, your pricing approach, your niche.

FAQ

GEO & AEO Services questions

What is the difference between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO is mainly about ranking web pages in search results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is about being used or cited by generative AI tools such as ChatGPT or Perplexity. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is about winning the direct answer, including Google AI Overviews and featured-style answers. In practice they overlap, and good technical SEO is still the foundation.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT or Google will recommend me?

No. These systems choose sources using their own models, and they change. I can make your business much easier to understand, verify and cite. I cannot honestly sell a guaranteed mention in ChatGPT or a fixed AI Overview placement.

Do I still need normal SEO?

Yes. If your site is slow, unclear, weakly structured or hard to index, AI tools will struggle as well. GEO and AEO work best on top of solid SEO services and clear website pages.

What is llms.txt?

It is a plain-text file at the root of your site that summarises who you are, what you offer and which pages matter, in a format large language models can read easily. Anmol Gupta already maintains one for this site, and I can add and maintain one for yours.

Does this help in India, the USA and the UAE?

Yes. People in all three markets now ask AI tools for local and industry recommendations. Content, examples and business details need to make your geography and service area explicit, otherwise the model has no reason to mention you for a Delhi, Dubai or US-city query.
