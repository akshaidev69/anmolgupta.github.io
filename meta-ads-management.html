📲 Meta Ads Management

Facebook and Instagram ads in India, USA and the UAE that turn attention into leads

Meta Ads management is the planning and running of paid Facebook and Instagram campaigns to get messages, leads or sales, including audiences, creatives, tracking and weekly optimisation. Anmol Gupta, AI engineer and developer, manages Meta Ads for businesses in India, the USA and the UAE, with ad creatives, an account in your name, and a media budget you pay directly to Meta.

I plan and run Facebook and Instagram campaigns around a clear offer: messages, lead forms, calls or sales. Creatives and tracking are part of the work, the ad account stays in your name, and I report cost per result honestly rather than promising a fixed number of leads.

Why Meta Ads still work when they are run carefully

Facebook and Instagram can reach people who are not searching yet but who match the life stage, job, city or interests of your best customers. That is useful for local services, coaching, real estate, e-commerce and many B2C offers. It is also easy to waste money if the offer is vague, the creative is generic, or you are optimising for cheap form fills that never answer the phone.

I run Meta Ads with a narrow first brief: one offer, one primary action, a test budget, and tracking that records a real enquiry. We expand only when the cost of a conversation makes sense.

Creatives, messages and lead quality

On Meta, the creative is often the targeting. A clear photo or short video, a specific promise, and a next step that feels easy will usually beat a clever slogan. I design creatives as part of the work. For many businesses in India and the UAE, a WhatsApp or Messenger objective is more natural than a long form. For higher-ticket US offers, a landing page with a calendar or a short form can produce better-quality leads.

Lead forms inside Instagram or Facebook are convenient, but they need tight questions and fast follow-up. Otherwise you pay for names that go cold in an hour. If follow-up is slow, we fix that process before scaling spend.

How this sits with the rest of your marketing

Paid social works better when the page looks active and the website is trustworthy. That is why this service often sits beside social media management, Google Ads or a landing-page update. AI ads can speed up creative variants and testing once the first offer is proven. They are not a substitute for a clear message.

India, the USA and the UAE

Audience behaviour and costs differ by country. A campaign that works in an Indian metro will not automatically work in New York or Dubai at the same budget. Language, currency, creative tone, privacy prompts and even which placement performs can all change. Campaigns are built per market, with billing in your own Meta account.

Accounts, reporting and an honest stop line

You own the Business Manager, the pixel and the page. Reports show spend, results and cost per result without hiding poor periods. If a test does not work, I will say so and recommend stopping or changing the offer rather than “optimising” a campaign that has already told us the answer.

If you want to test Facebook or Instagram ads, message Anmol Gupta with your offer, the country you want customers from, and a monthly budget range. You will get a clear plan, creatives, and a setup inside an account that stays yours.

FAQ

Meta Ads Management questions

What is the difference between Meta Ads and social media marketing?

Meta Ads is the paid Facebook and Instagram part. Social media marketing can also include LinkedIn and a wider growth plan. Social media management is the organic posting and community work. Many businesses need a combination, but they are not the same service.

Can you guarantee leads from Facebook or Instagram?

No. Costs move with competition, creative fatigue and the offer. I guarantee professional setup, testing, tracking and transparent reporting, not a promised lead volume.

Do WhatsApp campaigns work in India and the UAE?

Often yes, because many customers prefer to message rather than submit a long form. We still have to watch lead quality, reply speed and tracking so you are not paying for empty chats.

Who owns the ad account and the page?

You do. Campaigns run from your Business Manager, and you can remove access at any time. Your media budget is paid to Meta from your billing.

Do you run US-targeted Meta campaigns?

Yes. Audiences, creatives, landing pages and time zones are planned for the country you want, including the USA and the UAE as well as India.
