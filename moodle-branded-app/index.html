Moodle branded app: your own LMS app on Google Play and the App Store

Quick answer

A Moodle branded app is a mobile app published under your own name and icon on Google Play and the App Store, so learners log in to your Moodle site without searching for it. You can get one through Moodle's official Branded Moodle App service or a custom app built on Moodle web services. Anmol Gupta helps institutes choose and build the right route.

If your learners still have to download the generic Moodle app and type your site address, you are losing trust and logins. A branded app fixes that. Here is how the options compare, what you need to start, and how I can help.

What a Moodle branded app is

Moodle already has an official mobile app, and it is free for learners. The catch is that it is Moodle-branded. Learners have to find the app, search for your site by name or address, and then log in. A branded Moodle app removes those steps. It carries your institute's name, icon and colours, appears in the stores under your listing, and opens straight to your login screen.

For coaching institutes, schools, universities and corporate trainers, that small change makes the platform feel like part of your brand, and it makes onboarding easier on a phone, which is where most Indian learners study.

Three ways to get your own Moodle app

1. The standard Moodle app. Free and already compatible with most Moodle sites. Learners get course content, messaging, the calendar and the gradebook. Moodle's Premium plan, listed at about €499 per year, unlocks unlimited push notifications and offline learning. You do not get your own store listing.

2. The official Branded Moodle App service. Moodle customises its Premium app and publishes it under your Apple and Google developer accounts, with your name, icon, interface branding and push notifications. It stays aligned with Moodle app updates. The price is not published, so you ask Moodle or a reseller for a quote.

3. A custom app on Moodle web services. A developer builds your own app, for example in Flutter, that talks to your Moodle site. You control every screen and can add features the standard app lacks, such as a simplified home screen or links to your other products. The trade-off is that you also own the maintenance.

How to choose

  • Choose option 1 if you only need courses on mobile and are happy with the Moodle look.
  • Choose option 2 if you want a trusted, fully featured Moodle app under your brand and prefer Moodle to maintain it.
  • Choose option 3 if your learner experience is unusual, you want one app for several services, or you have a budget and team to maintain it.

What you need before you start

  1. A Moodle site on HTTPS with mobile web services enabled and a recent, supported version.
  2. Apple and Google developer accounts in your organisation's name.
  3. Your logo, icon, colours and app name, plus a privacy policy URL for the store listings.
  4. A test group of learners and teachers, so the first release is checked on real phones.

FAQ

Moodle branded app questions

What is a Moodle branded app?

It is a mobile app with your own name, icon, colours and login screen, published on Google Play and the App Store under your organisation's developer accounts. Learners sign in with their existing Moodle credentials and land straight on your site.

How much does a Moodle branded app cost?

Moodle lists its Premium app plan at about €499 per year and does not publish a price for the Branded Moodle App, so you must request a quote. Resellers publish their own figures, which vary. A custom app is quoted by scope after a free demo plan. Always ask for setup, yearly fees and update support separately.

Do I need Apple and Google developer accounts?

Yes. The app is published under your organisation's accounts. Apple's developer programme is $99 per year and Google Play charges a one-time registration fee, so budget for both in addition to the build.

Is the free Moodle app enough for my institute?

For many schools it is. The free Moodle app already gives learners access to courses, messages and the calendar. A branded app adds your own store listing, branding and more control over notifications, which matters when you want a professional learner experience.

Can you build a custom Moodle app instead?

Yes. A custom app can connect to your Moodle site through its web services, with your own design and extra features. It gives the most freedom but needs ongoing maintenance as Moodle versions change, so I will tell you honestly if the official route fits better.
