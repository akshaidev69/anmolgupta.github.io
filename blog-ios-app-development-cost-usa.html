iOS app development cost in the USA (2026): real USD ranges, timelines and how to save

Anmol Gupta · AI Engineer & Full Stack Developer
24 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

If you run a business or startup in the USA and want an iPhone app, the first quote you get can be confusing. One agency says $25,000, another says $250,000, and a freelancer on a marketplace says $3,000. All three can be "right", because they are pricing very different apps.

This guide breaks down iOS app development cost in the USA in 2026 in US dollars, explains what actually moves the price, and shows how US founders keep quality high while spending less.

Quick answer: how much does an iOS app cost in the USA?

A simple iOS app usually costs about $8,000 to $30,000. A typical business app with login, payments, an admin panel and a backend falls around $30,000 to $90,000. Complex apps with real-time features, AI or heavy integrations can pass $120,000. Hiring a skilled remote developer often cuts these figures by 40 to 60 percent.

These are typical market ranges, not fixed prices. Your exact number depends on scope, design and who builds it.

What drives iOS app development cost?

Cost is mostly time, and time comes from scope. These are the factors that matter most.

  • Number of screens and user flows. A 10-screen booking app is a very different job from a 60-screen marketplace.
  • Backend and admin panel. Most real apps need a server, a database and a dashboard where you manage users, orders or content. This is often 30 to 40 percent of the total effort.
  • Integrations. Stripe or Apple Pay, Google Maps, Twilio SMS, HubSpot or Salesforce, calendars and shipping APIs each add work.
  • Custom design. A polished, custom UI with animations costs more than a clean design built on Apple's Human Interface Guidelines.
  • Real-time and offline features. Chat, live tracking and offline sync are harder to build and test.
  • AI features. Chatbots, document reading, recommendations or voice features add model costs and engineering time.
  • Compliance. Health apps under HIPAA or finance apps with extra security reviews need more care and testing.

iOS app cost by type (USD, 2026)

Here is how common app types tend to price out when built properly, with a backend and App Store launch included.

  • Simple informational or utility app: $8,000 to $25,000, about 4 to 8 weeks.
  • Booking or appointment app for salons, clinics, gyms or home services: $20,000 to $50,000, about 8 to 12 weeks.
  • Ecommerce or ordering app with cart, Apple Pay and order tracking: $30,000 to $80,000, about 10 to 16 weeks.
  • On-demand or marketplace app with two user types and live tracking: $60,000 to $150,000+, about 4 to 7 months.
  • SaaS companion or internal business app for field teams or CRM data: $25,000 to $70,000, about 8 to 14 weeks.
  • AI-powered app with chat, image or document features: $35,000 to $120,000, depending on how deep the AI goes.

Who should build your iPhone app?

You have three realistic options in the USA, and each has a clear trade-off.

US agency. Typical rates run from about $100 to $200 per hour. You get project managers and local meetings, but you pay for the overhead.

US freelancer. Usually $60 to $150 per hour. Good value if you find a strong one, but availability and backend skills vary.

Remote senior developer (for example from India). Often $25 to $60 per hour for experienced full stack and mobile developers. You get the same tools, Swift, Flutter, Firebase and AWS, at a much lower cost. The key is choosing someone who communicates clearly, overlaps with your working hours and gives you full code ownership.

Many US startups now use a remote developer for the build and keep product decisions in-house. That is usually where the 40 to 60 percent saving comes from.

Native Swift or Flutter: which is cheaper?

Native Swift (SwiftUI) gives the best iPhone performance and the deepest access to Apple features like widgets, HealthKit and Apple Watch. It is the right choice for iOS-only apps or apps that depend heavily on Apple hardware.

Flutter builds iPhone and Android apps from one codebase. If you need both platforms, Flutter usually saves 30 to 40 percent compared with building two native apps, and the result feels smooth for most business apps.

My simple rule: if more than 80 percent of your customers use iPhones and you need Apple-specific features, go native. If you want both stores at launch on a tight budget, go Flutter.

Hidden and ongoing costs to plan for

The build is not the only cost. Budget for these from day one.

  • Apple Developer Program: $99 per year to publish on the App Store.
  • App Store commission: Apple takes 15 to 30 percent of in-app digital purchases. Physical goods and services paid with Stripe are not subject to it.
  • Hosting and backend: often $20 to $300 per month for a small to mid-size app on Firebase, AWS or Render.
  • Third-party services: maps, SMS, email, push notifications and AI APIs usually bill per use.
  • Maintenance: plan roughly 15 to 20 percent of the build cost per year for iOS updates, bug fixes and small improvements.

How to reduce iOS app development cost without cutting quality

  1. Start with an MVP. Launch the smallest version that solves one real problem, then add features using real user feedback.
  2. Get a clickable prototype first. A Figma prototype catches expensive mistakes before any code is written.
  3. Use proven services. Firebase Auth, Stripe and ready-made admin tools save weeks of custom work.
  4. Choose Flutter if you need Android too. One codebase, two stores.
  5. Pay by milestones. Fund the project in stages tied to working features, not upfront.
  6. Insist on code ownership. Your Apple developer account, your repository, your cloud account.

How long does it take to build an iOS app?

A simple app takes about 4 to 8 weeks, a standard business app about 2 to 4 months, and a complex marketplace or AI app about 4 to 7 months. Apple's App Store review usually takes one to three days, so plan a small buffer before launch.

Questions to ask before you hire an iOS developer

  • Can I see live apps you have shipped on the App Store?
  • Will you build the backend and admin panel too, or only the app?
  • Native Swift or Flutter for my case, and why?
  • How will we communicate, and what hours overlap with my time zone?
  • Who owns the code, the Apple account and the servers?
  • What does maintenance cost after launch?

FAQ

What is the average cost of an iOS app in the USA?

Most US small business iPhone apps land between $30,000 and $90,000 with a US agency. With a skilled remote developer, the same scope often costs $15,000 to $45,000.

Is it cheaper to build an iOS app or an Android app?

Costs are similar. Building both natively roughly doubles the work, which is why many US businesses choose Flutter to ship iPhone and Android apps from one codebase.

Can I hire an Indian developer for a US iOS app?

Yes. Many US startups do. Choose a developer with live App Store apps, clear English, some overlap with US working hours and a contract that gives you full code ownership.

How much does it cost to maintain an iPhone app?

Plan about 15 to 20 percent of the original build cost per year, plus hosting, the $99 Apple fee and any third-party API usage.

Do I need a backend for my iOS app?

Almost always, if your app has user accounts, payments, bookings or content you update. Firebase or a custom Laravel or Node.js backend are common choices.
