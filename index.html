About

Code on one side, growth on the other.

I started freelancing in July 2021 and quickly learned that a good website is only half the job. The other half is getting the right people to see it. So I learned both.

Today I take a product from idea to launch: the frontend, the backend and database, the Android and iOS builds, and then the Meta and Google Ads campaigns, including US targeted ones, that bring in leads.

Right now I work at SB Tiger Pvt. Ltd. in Dehradun, after almost three years at KY Advisor LLP in Gurugram. I hold a B.Com from Delhi University and I'm currently pursuing my MCA at Chandigarh University.
