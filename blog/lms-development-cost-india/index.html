LMS development in India (2026): cost in INR, must-have features and custom vs Moodle

Anmol Gupta · AI Engineer & Full Stack Developer
23 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

Online learning in India is no longer a pandemic experiment. Coaching institutes sell recorded courses, schools run homework and tests online, ed-tech founders launch niche academies, and companies train staff across cities. All of them eventually hit the same question: should we build our own learning management system (LMS), and what will it cost?

This guide answers that in plain language. It covers what LMS development in India costs in 2026 in rupees, the features that actually matter for Indian learners, and how to choose between Moodle, a SaaS platform and a custom LMS.

Short answer: how much does LMS development cost in India?

A basic LMS set up on Moodle or a WordPress LMS plugin typically costs around ₹40,000 to ₹1.5 lakh. A custom LMS with a web app, payments and an admin panel usually falls between ₹2 lakh and ₹6 lakh. A full platform with Android and iOS apps, live classes, DRM-protected video and multi-institute support can go from ₹6 lakh to ₹15 lakh or more. Scope, not the city of the developer, decides where you land.

Who needs an LMS in India?

  • Coaching institutes for JEE, NEET, UPSC, CA, banking and skill courses that want to sell recorded lectures and run mock tests.
  • Schools and colleges that need assignments, attendance, online exams and parent communication in one place.
  • Ed-tech startups and creators launching paid courses under their own brand instead of a marketplace.
  • Companies running onboarding, compliance and sales training for teams spread across offices.

Each group needs a different mix of features, which is why one-size quotes are often misleading.

Must-have LMS features for Indian learners

Mobile-first experience

Most Indian students learn on an Android phone, often on mobile data. The LMS must load fast, work on low-end devices and ideally offer an app with offline downloads.

Secure video hosting

Course videos are your product. Use a video platform with encryption, signed URLs, watermarking with the student's name or phone number, and limits on simultaneous logins to reduce piracy.

Tests and question banks

Mock tests with timers, negative marking, sectional analysis and rank lists are essential for competitive exam coaching. Bulk question upload from Excel saves teachers hours.

Payments in INR

UPI, cards, net banking and EMI through gateways like Razorpay, Cashfree or PhonePe, with GST invoices generated automatically. Coupons and batch-wise pricing help with launches.

Live classes

Integration with Zoom, Google Meet or a built-in live module, with recordings saved to the course afterwards.

Admin, teacher and parent roles

Separate dashboards for admins, teachers, students and, for schools, parents. Reports on progress, attendance and revenue should be one click away.

Hindi and regional language support

If your audience studies in Hindi or a regional language, the interface and content should support it from day one.

What drives LMS development cost in India

  • Platform approach: configuring Moodle or a WordPress plugin is far cheaper than a custom build.
  • Apps: adding Android and iOS apps can roughly double the budget compared with web only.
  • Video security: DRM and secure players add licence and integration costs.
  • Live classes and exams: a proctored exam engine or built-in live classroom is a large module on its own.
  • Integrations: CRM, WhatsApp notifications, SMS, payment gateways and existing ERP or school software.
  • Scale: a platform for 500 students has very different hosting needs from one for 50,000.
  • Design: a custom, branded UI costs more than a theme but builds trust and improves completion.

Do not forget running costs: hosting, video storage and bandwidth, SMS and WhatsApp messages, app store fees and maintenance. Budget roughly 15 to 20 percent of the build cost per year for upkeep.

Moodle vs SaaS LMS vs custom LMS

Moodle (open source)

Free to use, mature and packed with features for courses, quizzes and grading. It suits schools, colleges and corporate training. The trade-off is a dated interface out of the box, and it needs a developer who can theme it, host it well and keep plugins updated.

SaaS platforms

Ready-made Indian and global course platforms let creators start selling within days. You pay monthly fees or revenue share, and you are limited to their features, branding and data rules.

Custom LMS

Built with a stack like Laravel, Node.js or a headless setup with Flutter apps. It is right when you have a specific teaching model, need tight integration with your business, want full ownership of data and brand, or plan to scale into a real ed-tech product. It costs more upfront but has no per-student fees.

A practical rule: start with Moodle or SaaS to validate demand, then move to a custom LMS once you know exactly what your learners and teachers need.

A realistic timeline

  • Moodle or plugin-based setup: 2 to 4 weeks.
  • Custom web LMS (MVP): 6 to 10 weeks.
  • Web plus Android and iOS apps with live classes: 3 to 5 months.

Launching a focused MVP first, for example courses, tests and payments, then adding live classes and apps, keeps risk and cost under control.

How to choose an LMS developer in India

  • Ask for a live LMS or education project you can log in to, not just screenshots.
  • Check how they protect videos and handle concurrent logins.
  • Confirm who owns the source code, hosting accounts and app store listings.
  • Ask how the system will handle exam-day traffic spikes.
  • Get the maintenance plan and costs in writing before you start.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to start an LMS in India?

A Moodle or WordPress LMS setup on good hosting, with a branded theme and Razorpay payments, is usually the most affordable way to start.

Can I build an LMS app like a big ed-tech brand?

Yes, the core features can be built for a small institute. Start with an MVP and add advanced modules such as live classes and AI recommendations as revenue grows.

How do I stop students from sharing course videos?

Use encrypted video hosting with watermarking, restrict the number of devices per account and disable downloads outside the app. No system is perfect, but these steps cut piracy sharply.

Does my LMS need GST invoices?

If you are GST registered, yes. A good LMS generates GST-compliant invoices automatically for every purchase.

Can AI be added to an LMS?

Yes. Common uses include AI doubt-solving chatbots, automatic quiz generation from notes, and personalised revision plans based on test results.
